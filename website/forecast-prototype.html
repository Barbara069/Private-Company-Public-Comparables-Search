What drives the revenue path?

Paycom, Paylocity, ADP and Paychex each carry 25%. Full-year guidance is extended only through a stated 3% growth anchor and 0.6 persistence assumption. June/May fiscal years are month-weighted using uniform intrayear revenue. No five-year consensus estimates are invented.

Asure is a contextual fifth company, zero primary growth weight: its 2027 guidance includes a recent acquisition without a usable organic bridge. Removing Paylocity’s acquisition exposure and excluding the larger mixed-service peers remain important basket sensitivities.

Netchex’s own website signals

Mesh’s product launch predates the baseline and has no disclosed adoption/pricing bridge. The base case adds no numerical uplift. Bear/bull shocks are hypothetical sensitivities with a two-year half-life, not measured product effects. Repeated references to one event are deduplicated. Missing negative news is not proof of no downside.

Financial and valuation limits

The principal output has no dollar revenue, target EBITDA, target EV or equity value. The margin menu shows a conditional sensitivity only. Provider LTM EBITDA is not relabelled as adjusted EBITDA. Market multiples are dated references, not current prices. Private ownership does not establish an automatic discount; no equity DLOM is applied to EV, and no discount reduces revenue.
