What the test establishes

General business descriptions can be recovered without public-company filings. Reliable extraction requires subject/role checks, customer-versus-seller classification, and business-model gates before ranking. Evidence coverage is not correctness; no forecast accuracy, complete peer list, segment revenue mix or company financials are established by this pilot.
